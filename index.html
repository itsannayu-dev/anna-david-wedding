Our Story

Anna and David met in Grade 10 with a first date at a very questionable Chinese restaurant. From high school to the University of Waterloo, their paths have always stayed aligned. They love traveling the world together while searching for the best hikes and food spots.

Fifteen years later, they've grown up side by side, share a home together, and adopted an adorable little bichonpoo (Mimi). Now, they're ready to tie the knot and celebrate with their favourite people! ❤️

Travel

Travel from Toronto to the venue can be traffic heavy. Please allocate 1h 30m to 2h to get there. At night, when there's less traffic, it's about 1h 15min back to Toronto. If you're planning to enjoy the wine (and we hope you do!), please arrange a ride or consider staying the night nearby.

If you're flying in, the closest major airport is Toronto Pearson (YYZ) in Mississauga. From there, expect a similar drive to the venue as from Toronto.

Where to Stay

If you're looking for nearby accommodations, we recommend looking in St. Catharines or Hamilton, the two cities closest to our venue.

We'll be staying at the Four Points by Sheraton Hamilton, about 30 minutes away. Feel free to book there or explore other nearby options that work best for you.

Questions & Answers

Can I bring a plus one?

If your invitation includes a plus one, you'll see their name when RSVPing. Didn't receive one but hoping to bring someone? Reach out to us, we'll do our best.

When is the RSVP deadline?

Please RSVP by August 1, 2026, so we can have an accurate headcount.

Is parking available on site?

Yes, complimentary parking is available at the estate. If you plan to enjoy the wine, and we hope you do, please consider carpooling or arranging a ride. Note that it's a 1 hr 15 min drive back to Toronto.

What's the dress code?

Cocktail attire with a whimsical garden feel.

What will the weather be like?

High of 22°C, low of 15°C

Is the wedding indoors or outdoors?

Our wedding ceremony is outdoors, but our reception will be in a tent.

Is it okay to take pictures during the wedding?

We'd love for everyone to be fully present during the ceremony, so please keep phones tucked away. After that, snap away!

If you have any questions, feel free to message Anna or David.
